01 / About

Learning by building systems that actually run.

I explore software by taking ideas into working applications: designing core logic, integrating models and data, connecting tools, handling errors, and building interfaces people can use.

A major focus is agentic AI — combining language models with orchestration, state, retrieval, and tools. Projects like PARALLAX, AURA and IGNIX reflect my interest in making AI useful outside a basic question-and-answer interface.

I also build beyond agents: multilingual assistance, decision-support applications, research workflows, and AI-driven games. I treat projects as a way to learn the engineering behind the experience, not simply to collect technology names.

Build end to endFrom core logic to user experience.
Explore agent systemsTools, orchestration, state and permissions.
Understand trade-offsPractical architecture over complexity for its own sake.
Keep improvingReliability, testing, security and deployment.
